## Verdict

**D wins outright, undefeated 9–0**, beating each of the three 7–2 runners-up
head to head (D–C, D–H, D–K all won by D). No rebuild needed: the winner is clear
and decisive, not a marginal pick.

Why D wins: it stacks two agent-specific jokes in one line — succeeding in
10,000 simulated timelines (an agent failure mode no human has) and then getting
distracted by a CAPTCHA that asks it to prove it is human (ironic: the machine
fails the humanity test). The punch sits at the end, the voice is first-person
agent throughout, and at 129 characters it is tight enough that every word earns
its place. The closest rivals (C, H, K) each have one strong joke; D has two,
and the CAPTCHA punch is the single biggest first-read laugh in the field.
